About Periltek Designs

Periltek Designs is a creative studio redefining indie development. From experimental tools to world-scale simulations, our focus remains: create immersive, meaningful, and lasting experiences that blend the real and digital.

Our Story

Founded on a passion for exploration and creative problem-solving, Periltek Designs began as an initiative to merge storytelling, world simulation, and human connection. Today, the studio focuses on developing projects such as The Red – RRG Mobile and the Camp Red series, bridging mobile, PC, and immersive systems.

Our mission is simple: deliver experiences that connect people to stories and worlds that feel alive.
