Development Log Entries

Latest Updates

Recent milestones include a complete overhaul of The Red – RRG Mobile UI framework, improved asset streaming for Camp Red, and the finalized design pipeline for Rapid Red’s vehicle physics system.

We’ve also launched internal builds for Deep Red, focusing on atmospheric rendering and terrain persistence. The Darkening pre-production assets are now being shaped to define tone and scale.

Beta Testing Notes

Our next testing phase centers around The Red — early access participants will soon receive a public beta notification via our contact portal. Feedback channels will remain open through the integrated in-app feedback system.

For Camp Red, core system tests are progressing through closed network builds, ensuring proper synchronization and world state integrity before the open beta.

Team Insights

At Periltek Designs, every project stems from passion. Our small but dedicated team works in modular units — design, simulation, network, and narrative. Collaboration tools built in-house help us rapidly iterate and maintain creative flow.

As we expand, expect more frequent “micro-devlogs” featuring deeper dives into how we merge art, code, and storytelling into unified experiences.
