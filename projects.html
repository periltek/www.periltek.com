Project Portfolio Overview

Below you’ll find detailed summaries of the major projects currently active at Periltek Designs. Each one builds toward our vision of interactive worlds, meaningful player-experience, and creative growth.

Project Details

The Red – RRG Mobile

The Red – RRG Mobile is our flagship mobile application designed to bring the Red River Gorge to life. Features include interactive maps, live outdoor data, storytelling tied to physical location, and a community-driven layer of exploration.

Camp Red (Camping Simulator & Flagship Game)

Camp Red is our immersive camping simulation set in the Red River Gorge. It’s planned as a AAA-level experience offering realistic survival mechanics, environmental interactivity, and branching narratives.

Expansions:

  • Swift – a storyline expansion following the treasure of John Swift’s silver mines in the Red River Gorge.
  • Rapid Red – remote-control style expansion to Camp Red, offering RC vehicles, new terrain and challenges.
  • Deep Red – post-cataclysmic survival simulator building on the same universe after a major event.
  • The Darkening – a horror-experience iteration planned once the core sandbox is complete, set in the same world but focusing purely on high-tension horror.

Anarchy 404

Anarchy 404 is an experimental project from Periltek Designs that embraces open-world chaos, emergent systems, and community-driven narratives. The concept is built around the collapse of a digital society and players emerging in the “404” zone where law, code and infrastructure have broken down.

Key facets include:

  • Massive sandbox environment: Players navigate a shattered digital cityscape, scavenging, rebuilding and defining factions.
  • Emergent narrative architecture: No fixed story – players generate events, alliances, conflicts and bring meaning to the world.
  • Technical ambition: Dynamic world-modelling, server-scale simulation of infrastructure collapse, layered persistent world.
  • Community tools: Player-driven creation of factions, territories, trade and conflict systems, built-in mod / content pipelines.
  • Prototype status: Early stage, core engine work and simulation frameworks underway; full production planned once flagship titles move into final release phase.

At this time Anarchy 404 remains a visionary title – designed to expand Periltek’s reach into next-gen sandbox and persistent worlds.

We remain committed to innovation, ambition and quality across every title, and look forward to sharing development updates as we progress.
