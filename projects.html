Project Portfolio Overview

Below you’ll find detailed summaries of the major projects currently active at Periltek Designs. Each builds toward our vision of interactive worlds, meaningful player experience, and creative growth.

Project Details

The Red – RRG Mobile

The Red – RRG Mobile is our flagship mobile application designed to bring the Red River Gorge to life. Features include interactive maps, live outdoor data, storytelling tied to physical location, and a community-driven layer of exploration.

Camp Red (Camping Simulator & Flagship Game)

Camp Red is our immersive camping simulation set in the Red River Gorge. Planned as a AAA-level experience offering realistic survival mechanics, environmental interactivity, and branching narratives.

Expansions:

  • Swift – storyline expansion following John Swift’s silver mines.
  • Rapid Red – remote-control style expansion with new terrain and challenges.
  • Deep Red – post-cataclysmic survival simulator in the same universe.
  • The Darkening – horror-experience iteration set in the same world.

Anarchy 404

Anarchy 404 is an experimental project embracing open-world chaos and community-driven narratives in a collapsed digital society.

  • Massive sandbox environment
  • Emergent narrative architecture
  • Dynamic world modelling
  • Community tools
  • Prototype status: early stage engine and framework

We remain committed to innovation, ambition, and quality across every title, and will continue sharing development updates as we progress.
